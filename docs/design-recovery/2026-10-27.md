## Repository-specific records

### D33 — Adrien's Farm: ecological understanding through sensory story

**Use when:** When recovering the children's storybook, illustration system, character/world consistency, interactive editions, or ecological learning.

[Recovered intent] The storybook treats ecological literacy as lived encounters: compost, fungal threads, seeds, rain, trees, pollinators, rest, and reciprocity. Its method is wonder and noticing rather than a lecture disguised as dialogue.

[Design DNA] Preserve sensory scene descriptions, character consistency, season-specific atmosphere, repeated relational details, and space for a child to infer. The README's fictional protagonist is seven; that story fact must never overwrite the real child's age or personal record.

[Observed boundary] The repository describes early story/illustration pipeline work. A description of AI/human editorial stages is not proof of a complete illustrated book or tested learning outcome.

[Repair resolution — proposed] Separate story canon, scene beats, character references, illustration recipe/version, generation candidates, editorial selections, rights, and page composition. Keep ecological metaphors distinguishable from scientific assertions. Print, audio, and interactive versions should preserve the same core story while adapting pacing.

[Reimagined horizon] A coherent storyworld and reproducible illustration practice with optional participatory observations, printable activities, and gentle interactive editions.

[Carry-forward test] A short chapter works as a printed sequence and read-aloud without explicit moral exposition; images preserve character/material consistency; a factual editorial pass checks the ecological claims.
